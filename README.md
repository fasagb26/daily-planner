# Daily Planner Agent

A free planner that puts your calendars, Canvas assignments, sleep and the day's news in one place. It also flags problems before they happen, like double-bookings, deadlines during trips and exams with no study time planned.

**Open it:** https://fasagb26.github.io/daily-planner/

## How to use
1. Open the link above. It starts on the **⚙️ Setup** tab.
2. Enter your name and city.
3. Connect your Google Calendar, Canvas, Outlook or iCloud calendar (.ics link or file).

## Privacy
Everything you enter is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded to this site.
