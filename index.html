2. Connect your calendars

Works with any calendar that can export an .ics link or file: Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple iCloud, Canvas and more. Add as many as you like.

How do I get my link?
Google Calendar: Settings → click your calendar on the left → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.
Canvas: Calendar → Calendar Feed (bottom right) → copy the link.
Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → copy the ICS link.
Apple iCloud: Calendar app → right-click a calendar → Share → Public Calendar → copy the link.
Keep these links private Anyone with the link can see that calendar.
and/or upload a file:
Many calendar sites block browsers from reading the link directly. If that happens, open the link in a new tab to download the .ics file, then upload it. Re-upload whenever you want fresh data.
